PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 2026

M-CAS PRIVACY

m-cas collects nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

RECORDINGS

Everything you record is saved as audio files on your iPhone, in m-cas's folder in the Files app. It isn't uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them unless you've copied them out.

MICROPHONE

The microphone is used only while you're recording, and only to record onto your tape.

LOCATION

When you start a recording, m-cas asks for your location once to name the clip after where you are ("Trastevere, Rome"). That lookup uses Apple's Maps services on your device's behalf; m-cas doesn't store your location history or send it anywhere else. Names you give places are kept on your phone.
