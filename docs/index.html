M-CAS

A tape recorder for iPhone. Record a moment and it becomes a clip named for where you were and when. Every clip on a tape plays as one continuous tape you wind through with your thumb.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Deck: hold the cassette to record, drag it to wind, tap it to see the tape as a waveform. ▶ plays; ◀◀ and ▶▶ skip a clip on a tap and wind while held.
  • Shelf: keep as many tapes as you like. + NEW starts one; the gear opens settings.
  • Clips: the tape runs past the head. Tap a clip to cue it, hold one to rename its place, FULL plays the whole original.
  • Edit: trim clips and record over the tape from a mark. Nothing is ever deleted: the originals stay whole.
  • Action Button: bind the "Record a moment" shortcut to record without opening the app.

YOUR RECORDINGS

Tapes are folders of WAV files in the app's folder in the Files app, so you can copy them anywhere.
