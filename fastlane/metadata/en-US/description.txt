m-cas is a tape recorder. Record a moment — a room, a street, rain on the window, a friend laughing — and it becomes a clip named for where you were and when. Every clip on a tape plays back to back as one continuous tape.

WIND IT WITH YOUR THUMB
Drag the cassette to wind through the tape and hear it as you go, forwards or backwards, at the speed of your hand. Or push the shuttle and let it run.

TAPE-DECK KEYS
Chunky keys that clunk down. ▶ and ● latch while they're on. Tap ◀◀ or ▶▶ to skip a clip, hold to wind.

NAMED FOR WHERE YOU WERE
Clips are named for the place you're in — the bar, the park, the restaurant — or for places you name yourself, like Home.

RECORD OVER THE TAPE
Set a spot and record over it, the way you would on a cassette. The EDIT tab shows the tape as waveform: trim clips with handles, see what's been covered, and play any original in full. Nothing is ever deleted.

A SHELF OF TAPES
Keep a tape for a trip, one for home, one for the year. Give each a pattern, colors, a name, and draw on its label.

LIVE ACTIVITY AND LOCK SCREEN
Recording shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen with a Stop key. Playback shows with your tape as the artwork.

DRAWN IN SIXTEEN COLORS
Every screen is drawn in a dithered 16-color palette, pixel for pixel, on a starry sky, plain black or true OLED black.

Your recordings stay on your phone, as WAV files you can find in the Files app. No account, no ads, nothing collected.
